Adding up the months from **January to July**, the number of cattle slaughtered in slaughterhouses in Portugal was:

- 2014: 191,450
- 2022 (recent peak): 253,290
- 2024: 235,370
- 2025: 215,010
- **2026: 199,350**

It is the lowest figure for these seven months since 2014 and stands **15,660 head below** 2025 (−7.3%). Compared with 2022, the decline is 21%. July 2026 (32,090 head) is the latest month published by Eurostat.

**What this shows — and what it does not show.** The figures count head slaughtered in slaughterhouses in the country, not production in tonnes or meat consumption. They do not say the cause of the decline. They coincide with the decrease in the cattle herd and with the record sales of live cattle to Spain in 2025, but coinciding is not proving a link — it is one of the questions the Observatory will test with data.

The values are published by Eurostat in thousands of head, with rounding, and may be revised; any revision is recorded in our history.
