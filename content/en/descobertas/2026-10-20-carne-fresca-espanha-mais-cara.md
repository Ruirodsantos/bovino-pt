Adding up the twelve months of each year, Portugal's **imports of fresh or chilled beef** (Combined Nomenclature code 0201) from Spain were:

| Year | Value | Quantity | Average value per kg* |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2005 | €99.7 M | 32.5 thousand t | €3.07 |
| 2023 | €406.6 M | **74.4 thousand t** (series high) | €5.47 |
| 2025 | **€483.3 M** (series high) | 66.2 thousand t | €7.30 |

\* Our calculation: declared value divided by declared quantity. Current euros, not adjusted for inflation.

Between 2023 and 2025 the value rose 19 % and the quantity fell 11 %. The trend continues in 2026: from January to August Portugal paid €322.5 M for 42.2 thousand tonnes (€7.65/kg), against €313.7 M for 44.4 thousand tonnes in the same period of 2025.

**What this shows — and what it doesn't.** It shows that Portugal is paying more for each kilo of fresh beef it buys from Spain, and buying fewer kilos. It does not show why. The average value per kilo mixes different cuts, carcasses and qualities: it may reflect higher prices, a change in the kind of meat imported, or both. Nor does it say whether consumption in Portugal fell — purchases may have shifted to other countries or to domestic production. Those readings belong to the Observatory.

The figures are published by Eurostat from statistical trade declarations and may be revised; any revision is recorded in our history. Frozen beef (code 0202) is not included.
