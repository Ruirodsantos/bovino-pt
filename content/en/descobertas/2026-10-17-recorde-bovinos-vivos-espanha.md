Adding up the twelve months of 2025, Portuguese exports of **live cattle** (code 0102 of the Combined Nomenclature) to Spain were worth **€93.3 million**. It is the highest annual value since 2005, the first year we collected:

- 2005: €7.8 million
- 2015 (previous peak): €40.8 million
- 2021 (recent low): €12.2 million
- 2024: €58.0 million
- **2025: €93.3 million**

Between January and August 2026 the cumulative value is €38.8 million.

**What this shows — and what it does not yet show.** These figures measure the *declared value* of live animals that left Portugal for Spain. On their own, they do not say why the value soared: prices per animal may have risen, or the number of animals, or both. Nor do they say who benefits. Those are precisely the questions of the Observatory. The next analyses will separate price and quantity and compare with producer prices in the two countries.

The values are published by Eurostat from statistical trade declarations. Eurostat may revise them, and any revision is recorded in our history.
