In the November–December survey, the number of cattle in Portugal was:

- 2021: 1,640,650
- 2022: 1,579,140
- 2023: 1,527,720
- 2024: 1,486,910
- **2025: 1,472,090**

In four years, the country lost **168,560 head** (−10.3%), with declines in every year.

These data do not explain the cause. Drought, production costs and external demand for live animals are hypotheses we will test with data, without taking them as given.
