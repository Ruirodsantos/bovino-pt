According to the Directorate-General for Food and Veterinary Affairs (DGAV) and the Confederation of Portuguese Farmers (CAP), Portugal has **61 native breeds**: 15 of cattle, 16 of sheep, 6 of goats, 3 of pigs, 6 of equids, 4 of poultry and 11 of dogs.

The 15 cattle breeds are: Alentejana, Algarvia, Arouquesa, Barrosã, Brava de Lide, Cachena, Garvonesa, Jarmelista, Marinhoa, Maronesa, Mertolenga, Minhota, Mirandesa, Preta and Ramo Grande.

The number (15) comes from the official note. The spelling of each name is being checked against the catalogue itself. Each breed will get its own profile here, built from official sources, and we only publish what has been verified.
