According to the Eurostat farm structure survey, in **2023** Portugal had these cattle numbers by region (NUTS 2, the division in force since 2024):

- **Alentejo: 591,930** (41.1%)
- Norte: 293,000 (20.4%)
- **Autonomous Region of the Azores: 270,030** (18.8%)
- Centro: 139,460 (9.7%)
- Oeste e Vale do Tejo: 93,080 (6.5%)
- Península de Setúbal: 24,390 (1.7%)
- Grande Lisboa: 13,790 (1.0%)
- Algarve: 10,400 (0.7%)
- Autonomous Region of Madeira: 3,630 (0.3%)

The nine regions add up to **1,439,710 head**. The Alentejo and the Azores alone account for 861,960 — **59.9%** of the total. In the regions whose boundaries did not change, comparison with the 2020 survey shows the Azores falling from 282,820 to 270,030 (−12,790, −4.5%) and the Norte from 305,050 to 293,000 (−3.9%).

**What this shows — and what it does not show.** It shows where the cattle were in 2023, not where they are slaughtered or where the meat is consumed. The Alentejo and Centro of 2023 are not comparable with those of 2020: the 2024 NUTS 2 division redrew these regions and that of Lisbon, creating Oeste e Vale do Tejo, Grande Lisboa and Península de Setúbal. This farm survey is different from the November–December survey used for the national herd (1,527,720 cattle in 2023), so the two totals do not match. The values may be revised by Eurostat; any revision is recorded in our history.
