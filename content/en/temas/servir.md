- title: A good product is lost on a bad plate.
  text: A good product, aged and cooked with rigour, is lost on a cold plate or on a piece cut in the wrong direction.
  photo: servir-cena
- title: Before serving.
  text: '- **Warm plates**, not cold. Meat served on a cold plate cools in minutes.

    - **Resting finished.**

    - **Flaky sea salt** or coarse salt at the last moment, for crunch and contrast.

    - **The piece''s own fat**: on aged pieces, the melted covering fat can accompany the dish.'
- title: How to carve?
  text: 'Large pieces, such as certain rib steaks or thick slices, can be presented whole and carved in front of the customer, depending on the service concept.


    When that is the case: strips of **1 to 1.5 cm, against the grain**, with the bone on the plate for presentation.'
- title: And what to serve with it?
  text: 'Quality meat calls for simple accompaniments that do not hide it:


    - Potato (roasted over wood, boiled in its skin, or fried in olive oil).

    - Grilled vegetables or a salad of bitter leaves.

    - Short sauces: a reduction of the pan juices, herb butter, mustard.

    - For stewing cuts: mash, *migas*, oven rice, turnip tops (*grelos*).'
- title: The standard Bovino Português aims for.
  text: 'The restaurant El Capricho, in Jiménez de Jamuz (León), serves as a reference for ambition: an animal raised with a purpose, long ageing, a single well-worked piece. Bovino Português wants to bring together, in Portuguese, the knowledge needed to reach that level with Portuguese meat: native breeds, breeders, ageing, cuts and cooking.


    That demands work that cannot be done in a text: tastings, measurements, visits to breeders and ageing chambers, conversations with chefs and butchers. They are planned in the project''s research list and will be published only once verified.'
