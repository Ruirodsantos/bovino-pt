- title: How is it with our breeds?
  text: '- **[Barrosã](../../racas/barrosa/)**: Extensive system, in the mountains and mid-slopes, making use of carqueja and gorse. The production area of Carne Barrosã DOP covers 21 municipalities in the North.

    - **[Arouquesa](../../racas/arouquesa/)**: Systems vary: grazing on common land (rare today), permanent housing, housing only in winter, or a semi-extensive regime.

    - **[Maronesa](../../racas/maronesa/)**: Semi-housing and grazing for adults; permanent housing, fed at the manger, for the young.

    - **[Alentejana](../../racas/alentejana/)**: Continental climate, little rain and dry summers: poor pastures have shaped a robust animal. Carnalentejana DOP covers the districts of Beja, Évora and Portalegre and neighbouring municipalities.

    - **[Marinhoa](../../racas/marinhoa/)**: The most traditional system is intensive and outdoors: grazing for some months of the year and a daily return to the barn.


    *Still to be documented:* the average farm size and the weight of intensive dairy systems.'
