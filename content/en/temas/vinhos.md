scenes:
- title: The principle.
  text: '**Lean, tender** meat calls for finer wines. **Fatty, intense** meat calls for more body and tannins.'
- title: Tenderloin, rump, lean steaks.
  text: Medium-bodied reds, such as elegant Douro or Dão, or Bairrada made from Baga with good acidity.
- title: High loin, rib steak, aged thick-cut steak.
  text: Structured reds from the Douro or the Alentejo, preferably with a few years behind them.
- title: Stews and working-muscle meat.
  text: Reds with body and good acidity. The wine from the stew itself is usually the best accompaniment.
note: Suggestions in principle, yet to be validated by a sommelier.
