- title: Where does the flavour live?
  text: In the fat. But meat holds **three different fats**, and a professional tells them apart. Each does a different job.
- title: 'The outer one: covering fat.'
  text: 'This is the fat on top of a loin, for example. It should be firm and range from white to cream.


    A strong yellowish tone is common in older, grass-fed animals, because of the carotenoids in grass. **It is not a defect**: it is, in fact, a sign of a grass-fed diet.'
- title: 'The middle one: intermuscular fat.'
  text: It sits between the muscles. It **melts and lubricates** during cooking.
- title: 'The inner one: marbling.'
  text: 'This is intramuscular fat: the fine white threads inside the muscle.


    It counts **most towards the flavour and juiciness** of a grilled steak.'
  photo: gordura-cena
- title: Why do some cuts marble more?
  text: 'Marbling varies greatly with breed, age and rearing system.


    And [ageing](../maturacao/) heightens the flavour that the fat carries.'
